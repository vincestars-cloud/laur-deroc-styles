Therapeutic Services Tailored
to Your Unique Journey

01

Addiction

One-on-one sessions designed to help you understand yourself more deeply, process difficult emotions, and build the skills to navigate life's challenges with confidence and clarity.

02

Couples Therapy

Using evidence-based Gottman Level 2 methodology, we work together to rebuild trust, improve communication, and restore the connection that brought you together in the first place.

03

EMDR Therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing is a powerful, research-backed approach for healing trauma, PTSD, anxiety, and distressing memories without relying solely on talk therapy.

04

IFS Therapy

Internal Family Systems therapy helps you identify and understand the different "parts" of yourself — including the wounded parts that drive self-sabotage — so you can lead from your core Self.

05

Hypnotherapy

Certified Clinical Hypnotherapy accesses the subconscious mind to break long-standing patterns, reduce anxiety, and facilitate deep healing that conscious willpower alone often cannot reach.

06

Trauma Therapy

For those experiencing flashbacks, nightmares, intrusive thoughts, or emotional detachment — specialized trauma care to help you reclaim safety.

About Lauren

About Lauren DeRossette

Lauren DeRossette is a Licensed Professional Counselor based in Brookhaven, Georgia with over nine years of clinical experience working with adults navigating trauma, relationship challenges, anxiety, depression, and substance recovery.

She brings a deeply integrative approach — weaving together EMDR, Internal Family Systems, Gottman Couples Therapy, and Clinical Hypnotherapy to meet each person exactly where they are. Her work is guided by a core belief that healing is not only possible, it is the natural direction of a life given the right support.

Lauren sees clients in person at her Brookhaven, GA office and virtually throughout the state of Georgia.
