Therapeutic Approaches

7 Modalities. One Integrated Path.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

IFS helps you understand and heal the different "parts" of yourself that carry burdens from past experiences. This gentle, non-pathologizing approach fosters deep self-compassion and lasting inner harmony.

EMDR Therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a highly effective, evidence-based treatment for trauma and PTSD. It helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they no longer hold power over you.

Clinical Hypnotherapy (CCH)

Hypnotherapy uses focused relaxation to access the subconscious mind, helping to break through deeply ingrained patterns, reduce anxiety, and accelerate therapeutic progress in a safe, guided way.

Somatic Approaches

Trauma lives in the body as much as in the mind. Somatic-informed therapy helps you recognize and release physical tension and stress responses, restoring a sense of safety and groundedness in your own body.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

CBT is a structured, goal-oriented approach that helps identify and change unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors. It is highly effective for anxiety, depression, and developing practical coping skills.

Gottman Method (Level 2)

The Gottman Method is a research-backed approach to couples therapy that strengthens friendship, manages conflict, and builds shared meaning. Lauren is trained to Level 2, offering deep couples work for lasting relationship change.
