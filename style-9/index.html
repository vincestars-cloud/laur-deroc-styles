Therapy for every chapter

Whether you're facing a life transition, relationship stress, or longstanding patterns — there's a path forward here.

Support built around you

Individual therapy is a space to slow down, make sense of what you're experiencing, and build the tools to move forward with clarity and confidence.

Working with adults navigating anxiety, depression, trauma histories, life transitions, and identity questions — with warmth, honesty, and proven clinical approaches.

  • Anxiety, depression, and mood management
  • Trauma processing with EMDR and IFS
  • Life transitions and identity exploration
  • Grief, loss, and major life changes
  • Burnout, people-pleasing, and boundary work

Rebuild connection that lasts

As a Gottman Level 2 trained therapist, Lauren works with couples to break destructive communication patterns, deepen emotional intimacy, and build lasting relationship skills.

Whether you're navigating conflict, infidelity recovery, or simply growing apart — couples therapy creates a structured path back to each other.

  • Communication and conflict resolution
  • Gottman Method — research-based couples work
  • Infidelity and trust repair
  • Premarital counseling and relationship prep
  • Intimacy and emotional connection

Navigate change as a family

Family systems hold patterns that can be difficult to see from the inside. With a compassionate, structured approach, family sessions help improve dynamics and create healthier ways of relating.

Supporting families through transitions, conflict, communication breakdowns, and the effects of trauma on family systems.

  • Family systems and communication patterns
  • Blended family dynamics and adjustment
  • Parent-child relational challenges
  • Intergenerational trauma exploration
  • Support through major family transitions
