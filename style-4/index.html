About Lauren

Nine Years. 500+ Adults. One Core Belief: Healing Is Possible.

Lauren DeRossette is a Licensed Professional Counselor, National Certified Counselor, and Certified Clinical Trauma Professional based in Brookhaven, Georgia. She brings over nine years of clinical experience and a deeply integrative approach to therapy — combining EMDR, IFS, Gottman Couples work, and Clinical Hypnotherapy to meet each person exactly where they are.

Her practice is grounded in the belief that every person holds within them the capacity to heal — and that with the right support, growth is not just possible, it's inevitable.
