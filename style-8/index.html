Meet Your Therapist

A Compassionate Guide on Your Journey

Lauren DeRossette is a Licensed Professional Counselor based in Brookhaven, Atlanta, serving clients in-person and virtually across Georgia. With advanced training in EMDR, Internal Family Systems, and Gottman Level 2 couples therapy, she works with adults navigating trauma, anxiety, relationship ruptures, and identity transitions.

Lauren's approach is direct but warm — she won't let you stay stuck in circles, but she'll never push you somewhere you aren't ready to go. Her work is grounded in the belief that healing happens in relationship: the one you build in the therapy room.

Common Questions

If you don't see your question here, call — Lauren picks up.

How long does a typical session last?
Individual sessions are 50 minutes. Couples sessions run 75 minutes. EMDR intensives can be scheduled as 90-minute extended sessions.
What if I've never been to therapy before?
Perfect. Most clients say the first session is nothing like they expected — less formal, more conversational. The only goal is for you to feel heard and to decide if it's the right fit.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes — a 15-minute phone consultation at no charge. It's a chance to ask questions and see if Lauren's style and your needs are a good match before committing.
Is virtual therapy as effective as in-person?
For most presenting concerns — including EMDR — yes. Research supports telehealth as equally effective. In-person is available in Brookhaven for clients who prefer face-to-face.

Ready to Take the First Step?

A 15-minute free consultation — no pressure, no forms, no jargon. Just a conversation about where you are and where you want to be.
