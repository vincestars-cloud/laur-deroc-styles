The Methods Behind the Work

Evidence-Based Therapeutic Approaches

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

IFS maps your inner world into distinct parts — each with its own role and history. By understanding these parts instead of fighting them, you build inner harmony and genuine self-leadership.

EMDR Therapy

EMDR uses guided eye movements to help the brain reprocess traumatic memories. What once felt overwhelming becomes something you can hold without being taken over by it.

Clinical Hypnotherapy

In a focused, relaxed state, the subconscious becomes receptive to change. Hypnotherapy helps release patterns rooted in early experience that conscious effort alone cannot shift.

Somatic Approaches

Trauma lives in the body as much as the mind. Somatic work helps you recognize and release physical tension, hypervigilance, and the body's stored stress responses.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

CBT identifies the thought patterns driving anxiety, depression, and avoidance — and builds practical tools to interrupt those cycles and respond differently.

Gottman Method (Level 2)

Grounded in decades of couples research, Gottman Method targets the Four Horsemen of relationship failure and replaces them with patterns that build lasting connection.
