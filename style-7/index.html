About Lauren

A space where you can

I'm Lauren DeRossette, LPC. I work with adults who are tired of just getting through the day — people ready to actually address what's holding them back, with real support and evidence-based tools.

I help adults navigate anxiety, trauma, depression, and life's major turning points using evidence-based approaches including EMDR, CBT, and mindfulness.
