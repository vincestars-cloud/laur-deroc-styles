EMDR Therapy

Heal the memories that
still hold power over you.

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a proven, structured approach to processing traumatic memories. It allows the brain to reprocess disturbing experiences so they no longer trigger the same emotional charge — creating relief that lasts.

  • Internationally recognized, research-backed protocol
  • Effective for single-event and complex, layered trauma
  • Often produces faster results than talk therapy alone
  • Minimal verbal disclosure required — the brain does the work

About Lauren

Lauren DeRossette is a Licensed Professional Counselor with over nine years of experience working with adults navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, and relationship challenges. Common Questions

Everything you need to know

No question is too small. Starting therapy is a big step — here are answers to the ones we hear most often.

Do you accept insurance?
Lauren is currently private pay only. Many clients use out-of-network benefits through their insurance provider — Lauren can provide a superbill to help you seek reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are also accepted.
How long does therapy typically take?
It depends on your goals and the depth of what you're working through. Many clients begin experiencing meaningful shifts in 8–12 sessions. Others continue for a year or more for deeper, lasting transformation. Lauren collaborates with you on your timeline throughout.
Are virtual sessions as effective as in-person?
Research shows that telehealth therapy is equally effective for most presentations, including trauma work and EMDR. Virtual sessions allow Lauren to serve clients across all of Georgia without requiring Atlanta-area proximity.
What is the first session like?
The first session is an intake — Lauren will ask about your history, what's bringing you in now, and what you hope to get from therapy. There's no pressure to dive deep immediately. It's as much about you deciding if Lauren is the right fit as it is about assessment.
Do you work with all relationship structures?
Yes, fully. Lauren works with couples in all configurations — married, unmarried, same-sex, polyamorous, and post-divorce co-parenting relationships. All identities and relationship structures are welcomed and affirmed.
