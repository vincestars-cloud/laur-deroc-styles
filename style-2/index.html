About Lauren DeRossette

A Compassionate Guide on Your Journey

Lauren DeRossette is a Licensed Professional Counselor, NCC, and CCTP-II with over 9 years of experience helping adults heal from trauma, manage anxiety and depression, navigate substance abuse recovery, and strengthen their relationships.

"Therapy is a journey into yourself. It is choosing to step forward into growth rather than shy back into what is safe and comfortable. Within each of us lies an innate goodness and a divine spark."

Lauren practices from a trauma-informed, strengths-based perspective, integrating multiple evidence-based modalities to offer truly personalized care. She serves clients in Brookhaven, GA and virtually throughout the state of Georgia.

Philosophy & Approach

Healing That Goes to the Root

True healing isn't just about managing symptoms — it's about understanding yourself at a deeper level and building a life that feels genuinely yours. Lauren's integrative approach combines evidence-based methods with genuine human connection.

Drawing from IFS, EMDR, Gottman Method, hypnotherapy, and somatic approaches, Lauren meets each client where they are and helps them chart a course toward lasting wellbeing. She specializes in trauma, anxiety, depression, substance abuse, and couples work.
