Meet Your Therapist

Lauren DeRossette, LPC

With over 9 years in practice and more than 500 adults served, Lauren brings a rare combination of advanced clinical training and genuine human warmth to every session. She specializes in trauma, addiction recovery, and relationship healing — using evidence-based modalities including EMDR, IFS, Gottman Method, and clinical hypnotherapy.

Lauren believes therapy is not about fixing what's broken — it's about discovering the strength that was always there. Her approach challenges you to grow, while holding space for every part of your journey.
